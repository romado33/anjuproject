# Support triage procedures

## Triage steps

1. Identify product line (TrialMaster, IRMS MAX, TA Scan) or mark as cross-product.
2. Classify issue type: bug, configuration, training, migration, data question, implementation, compliance.
3. Assess urgency based on customer impact and regulatory deadlines.
4. Pull relevant context from prior tickets and CRM account health.
5. Propose actions: Jira issue, Teams notification, Salesforce activity, NetSuite note if billable work, BambooHR onboarding task if staffing changes.

## Auditability

- Every routing decision must record reasoning and confidence.
- Approvals are required before any write to external systems of record.
